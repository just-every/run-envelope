# Payroll automation settings

The configured native owner opens **Automation settings** from a company's payroll settings. A weekly policy names an already reviewed pay group, its first seven-day period and pay date, first preparation instant and official cutoff with an explicit UTC offset, and responsible approver, backup and service duty contact. Names do not create grants or establish staffed service coverage.

The current implementation requires a currently authorized native person's **Process due work** action. It creates native immutable earnings drafts, never approvals. An occurrence's stable run reference and conditional version insert prevent a concurrent or restarted pass from making another draft. Existing source, exact review, human proof and payment-release restrictions remain in force. Missing inputs and tax calculation remain holds on the earnings preview. No payable payroll or payment receipt is generated.

The company API is at `./api/envelope/companies/<company>/automation`: GET returns policies and retained tasks; POST creates a policy. POST to its `./process` child processes due occurrences; POST to `./disposition` records one justified disposition with `policyId`, `occurrence`, `stage` and `reason`. API paths start with `/api/` at the installation origin. Configuration requires the configured native owner. Other native staff require explicit current company grants: `automation:inspect` to read policies and tasks, `automation:prepare` plus inspection and `rates:propose` to process, and `automation:dispose` to justify one disposition. Each operation also requires `records:read`, company-wide location scope and compensation access. Company-wide policies are refused for location-limited grants rather than exposing other locations; usable location-limited processing remains owed. Preparation grants do not grant disposition, configuration or payroll approval. Bearer agents gain no authority. Every database operation repeats current session and grant-version fences; revoking or changing the grant invalidates prior in-flight authority. Inspection and decisions retain attributed audit. Configuration and disposition reasons use the installation's encrypted custody key. Dispositions remain attributed and audited.

Each occurrence has separate hours, approval, backup and service tasks. Their stable composite keys deduplicate repeated passes. Backup tasks are due eight support hours before the cutoff and service tasks four support hours before it. Detection after a threshold creates the task immediately, retaining the original due instant. The support clock counts 08:00 to 20:00 in US Eastern time on weekdays, excludes Federal Reserve holidays and follows DST. The holiday basis is the [Federal Reserve Financial Services schedule](https://www.frbservices.org/about/holiday-schedules); Saturday holidays do not close the preceding Friday, and Sunday holidays close Monday. Deadlines repeat at the explicitly configured UTC instant, so this initial version does not infer new official deadlines or preserve a recurring local wall time across DST. Each future actual deadline still needs verification.

Tasks display **delivery unavailable**: no message was sent, and task existence is neither receipt nor human acknowledgement. Service coverage remains unverified. A justified disposition stops only the selected task, retaining its attribution, due date and delivery state. Processing never revives a disposed task. A disposition cannot approve a run or authorize release. Catch-up older than 52 weeks explicitly refuses for owner review rather than silently discarding obligations.

Still owed under ENV-046: background scheduling and operational lease recovery; prepared, failed and next-action monitoring; delivered reminders and visible failed or unknown delivery; automatic hours and current exact-run completion checks; authoritative per-occurrence deadlines across timezone changes; held tax-account deposit obligations, rate true-up and once-only remittance with exposure and fault history; minimum-wage escalation to an actually staffed service and a named-human-approved lawful payment received by payday. No automatic outbound action or staffed-service claim is enabled. Restored history confers no session or grant; operational adoption and complete privacy lifecycle proof remain owed with ENV-054 and ENV-056.

## Superseding policy ruling

CT policy ruling, 27 October 2026: APPROVE WITH RIDERS. The current configured native owner may explicitly process native preparation and private follow-up tasks through current company authority, with attributed configuration, processing and justified disposition. This is an interim authority boundary. Names entered as approver, backup or service contact create no grant, notification receipt or staffed coverage. Preparation and task disposition never approve a pay run, release money, submit a filing or authorize an outbound action. A disposition binds one identified obligation and retains its actor, time and reason; it cannot erase the obligation’s legal deadline, fault history or another task. The complete target retains durable background scheduling, scoped human and agent preparation, exact per-run human approval, completion-driven reminder cessation, actual notification outcomes and accountable staffed escalation. ENV-046 retains ENV-028 current scoped authority, ENV-034 exact human approval, ENV-035 uncertain effects, ENV-045 reviewed unchanged-run approval and ENV-056 operational recovery; their separate implementation duties remain with those clauses. Apply the eight and four support-hour rules to the earliest applicable actual cutoff in its official timezone, including held remittances after wages are paid. A missing tax account holds only the jurisdiction’s deposit and filing; it never justifies delayed wages. Minimum-wage escalation must still reach an accountable human and an evidenced lawful payment by payday. Task creation, provider boundaries and policy approval supply none of those outcomes. ENV-046 remains PARTIAL and every original acceptance sentence remains required.

The synchronized contract Gap remains the full target boundary. This increment proves only the bounded native delegation described above; it does not establish usable location-limited or agent preparation, background scheduling, delivered escalation or staffed resolution.

# Hawaii plan evidence and held share previews

Envelope retains private, effective-dated Hawaii TDI and Prepaid Health Care evidence for a named worker. The configured native owner can record an insurer, authorized benefits administrator or Willow source packet, its plan designation, premium invoice, eligibility evidence and applicable annual HC-5. This is native evidence entry: there is no connected insurer import, independent authentication of plan approval, determination of eligibility or insurance coverage.

Use the company's private API under `/api/envelope/companies/:companyId/people/:personId/hi-benefits`. GET returns retained plan and preview history. POST to its `plans` child records an immutable version. GET `plans/:planId/review` obtains a session-bound ten-minute exact review; POST `plans/:planId/approve` requires that version and review token. A changed invoice, eligibility record, waiver, person or plan version requires fresh review. Plan corrections retain the original evidence and require a reason. Staff and agent credentials receive no authority from this interim owner-only door; broader delegation remains owed.

Each plan records `program`, `ruleYear`, `effectiveFrom`, `effectiveThrough`, `source`, `approvalEvidence`, `eligibility`, `eligibilityEvidence`, `employeeBasisPoints`, `planType`, `dependentEmployerBasisPoints`, `waiver` and `premiums`. `premiums` retains actual attributable premium amounts and their evidence, plus explicit prior gross wages, employee collections, dependent collections and opening-register evidence. All amounts are integer cents. A TDI premium period identifies the start of one seven-day wage week. A PHC premium period identifies one calendar month. The evidence must allocate actual premium to that period; Envelope does not guess from an annual invoice or a pay frequency.

POST to `preview` with an object identifier, expected version, person identifier, exact plan identifier and version, wage evidence and ordered wage periods. Each period specifies its dates, pay date and gross wages; TDI also requires its designated `weekFrom`. Optional requested employee and dependent amounts must fit the remaining lawful ceiling. Multiple wage periods share their weekly or monthly accumulator within the proposal. Previously collected amounts come only from the reviewed opening register. Separate previews are alternatives: they do not increment collection balances, authorize deductions or represent settled payroll. Corrections must reconcile actual prior collection in fresh retained evidence. Overlapping wage periods and overlapping TDI premium weeks are refused.

The existing private company `calculation-preview` API also accepts this request as `hiBenefits`. Its evidence periods must match the calculation's gross, dates and Hawaii work state. The response adds the retained benefit proposal and preserves all existing calculation and jurisdiction holds. A benefit proposal is never a complete net paycheck.

For 2026 TDI the employee ceiling is the least of the reviewed employee cost share (at most half actual premium), 0.5 percent of cumulative weekly wages and $7.50 for the week, less prior collections. The employer may pay the entire cost. PHC caps employee-only coverage at the lesser of the reviewed share (at most half premium) and 1.5 percent of cumulative monthly gross, less prior collections. The employer owes the rest. Dependent premiums are shown separately under the plan's retained allocation. A 7(b) plan requires at least half dependent premium from the employer; a 7(a) plan uses its evidenced allocation. Dependent amounts cannot be silently added to the employee-only wage ceiling. Fractional-cent ceilings round down so a proposal never exceeds a statutory maximum.

A current reviewed HC-5 produces zero proposed collections and a waiver-evidence label, never a coverage claim. Missing plan, premium or eligibility evidence, an expired waiver, an unsupported rule year, an unreviewed correction or an above-cap request requires correction and returns no new proposal. A reviewed opening register that already exceeds the lawful ceiling also requires correction; this API does not fabricate a refund.

Evidence and proposals use the existing encrypted worker workspace, attributed audit, current-session and current-company fences, privacy inventory, eligible erasure and source replay fences. Correcting a source marks older pending proposals stale without overwriting historical amounts. Record history persists across restart. Restored review history is not renewed payment or provider authority.

Only the retained 2026 rule edition is currently supported. Actual approved-plan authenticity, complete eligibility and source custody, authorized provider synchronization, paid deductions, corrected paid stubs, insurer remittance and full historical effective rules remain unfinished. There is no automatic outbound action, coverage activation, payment, filing, hold release or erasure. Existing carrier, calculation-catalog and owner-authorized company and payment-rail boundaries continue to apply.

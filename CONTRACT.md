# Envelope — product contract

Target edition: **20 October 2026**. Envelope is full-service US small-business payroll: calculate what each person is owed, pay them on time, remit the taxes and deductions, file the returns, and carry every exception through to an evidenced outcome. A US employer switching from Gusto, ADP RUN, Paychex Flex, QuickBooks Payroll, Warp, Rippling Payroll or OnPay must be able to complete that job here. The first buyer has 1–100 employees; that starting buyer does not cap the product's growth or its coverage of states, local taxes, industries or payment methods.

This is the destination, not a release promise. There is no released payroll product; the Status table records the first partial record/access implementation and the remaining unproved behavior. Money movement, reporting-agent enrollment, tax engines, agency portals, provider applications, native clients and partner agreements are engineering obligations. A builder may use Check, Gusto Embedded, Salsa, Zeal or a sponsor-bank arrangement, but must deliver the named outcomes, expose unsupported provider coverage as unfinished, and never replace payment or filing with a generated document labeled complete. Envelope remains its own product; shared code does not merge payroll with HR, time, accounting or practice management.

## Acceptance conventions

Each clause below includes its own public-door journey; [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md) repeats the complete checklist. An acceptance journey uses actual application routes, documented API/MCP doors, the deployed Base runtime or supported mobile client. Every browser journey runs at **1440 and 390 CSS pixels** with realistic US company, hourly/salaried/tipped employee and contractor records, English/Spanish where relevant, keyboard navigation, loaded, empty, held, failed and completed states. At minimum the shared business fixture includes California hourly/final pay, Pennsylvania local taxes, a NJ-to-PA move, Colorado remote work, an employee with two garnishments, a near-limit retirement contributor and a contractor with prior-year records. Sensitive identifiers are synthetic only in tests.

Fakes belong only at the external network/paid edge and follow a named provider's official request, event, error and acknowledgement schema. Record the provider, schema edition and scenarios; a fake response is test evidence, never a production receipt. Use a provider/agency sandbox where available. Where an agency lacks one, test its official file/portal schema, rejection and reconciliation flow at the edge; production readiness additionally requires approved agency/provider access and a real authorized submission, acknowledgement or settlement appropriate to that channel. Do not make production payments for a test without the employer's exact authorization. Provider accepted, agency accepted, bank settled, employee received, recipient notified and human approved are separate facts. An upload, HTTP 200, generated file, timer expiry or model statement cannot stand for one of those outcomes.

All clauses inherit ENV-028 and ENV-034's current human authority and ENV-035's uncertain-effect handling. A person approves every pay run, money movement, signed filing, bank-detail change, garnishment setup and rate change. One review can authorize a listed batch of exact items and its funding/deposit schedule; it cannot authorize unknown future items. Agents may prepare and reconcile, never approve on a person's behalf. A revoked approver, changed evidence or changed payload invalidates pending authority. A failed optional provider remains visible while unaffected payroll work continues; missing required security/runtime configuration refuses activation of the affected service.
